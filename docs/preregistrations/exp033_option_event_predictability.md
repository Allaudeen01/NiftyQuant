## What this is (and is NOT)

A disciplined test of whether unusual, structurally-motivated NIFTY
option-chain events carry information about the **probability that NIFTY hits
a small profit target before a small adverse barrier** within a short holding
period — not a directional predictor, not a strategy-mining loop. Maximum
attainable verdict from the historical phase (Phases 2–10) is **CANDIDATE FOR
FORWARD TESTING**; nothing here can promote itself to live or paper capital.
The forward-testing loop (Phase 11+, Part 13 of the spec) is a **separate**
step gated on this historical phase completing honestly.

### Data-source-of-truth (locked, per Part 1)
| Purpose | Source | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Signal generation | `option_chain.spot`, per snapshot | ~2 min |
| Entry simulation | `option_chain.bid`/`ask` at entry snapshot | ~2 min |
| Target/stop simulation | `option_chain.spot`, walked snapshot-to-snapshot | ~2 min |

### Known field caveats (locked assumptions)
- `implied_volatility` is 100% NaN by design; IV is derived via
  `nifty_quant.research.derive_iv` / `analytics.black_scholes.implied_volatility`
  (r=0.065, q=0.012). Median derivable coverage 78.8% (range 69–83.8%) across

- `oi_change` (stored column) is **0% nonzero on every single day** — dead in

  consecutive snapshots per (strike, option_type, expiry) during feature

## Event universe v1.0 (LOCKED — bounded, versioned, no unlimited search)

**Scope:** relative strikes **ATM−2 … ATM+2** (5 levels, strike spacing = 50
pts per audit) × **{CE, PE}** = 10 tracked series per snapshot. All rolling
statistics are **same-day, causal, expanding-within-day** (minimum 10 prior
snapshots that day before a family can fire — cross-day time-of-day×strike
baselines are not used because 58 days ÷ 5 strikes ÷ 2 types ÷ ~10 time
buckets leaves too few observations per cell to be a reliable baseline; this
is stated up front, not discovered after a look-ahead bug).

| Family | Version | Definition | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** Volume Spike | v1.0 | z-score of 2-min volume increment vs same-day trailing rolling mean/std (window 20 snapshots ≈ 40 min) | \|z\| ≥ 2.5 |
| **E2** OI Expansion | v1.0 | z-score of derived OI change vs same-day trailing rolling mean/std (window 20) | z ≥ 2.0 |
| **E3** OI Unwinding | v1.0 | mirror of E2 | z ≤ −2.0 |
| **E4** Premium Momentum | v1.0 | same-day rolling percentile (window 30 ≈ 60 min) of the 2-min mid-price return | ≥ 90th pct (up) or ≤ 10th pct (down) |
| **E5** IV Shock | v1.0 | same-day rolling z-score of derived per-strike IV; `DISABLED` if IV unsolvable | \|z\| ≥ 2.0 |
| **E6** Combined | v1.0 | AND of two base triggers, same snapshot, same (strike, type) — see below | both legs fire |

**E6 combined events (exactly 6, the full universe — no other combination is
tested):**

| Code | Definition | Pre-declared NIFTY-direction hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| E6.1 | CE volume spike (E1) + CE premium momentum-up (E4) | BULLISH |
| E6.2 | PE volume spike (E1) + PE premium momentum-up (E4) | BEARISH |
| E6.3 | CE OI unwinding (E3) + CE premium momentum-up (E4) | BULLISH |
| E6.4 | PE OI unwinding (E3) + PE premium momentum-up (E4) | BEARISH |
| E6.5 | PE OI expansion (E2) + PE premium momentum-down (E4) | BULLISH |
| E6.6 | CE OI expansion (E2) + CE premium momentum-down (E4) | BEARISH |

Direction is a **declared prior**, exactly like Exp032's S1/S3 mirror-image
discipline — it can be REJECTED by the data, it is not fit to it.

Every event record stores: `event_id, timestamp, trading_date, spot,
relative_strike, absolute_strike, option_type, event_type, event_family_version,
event_strength (the triggering z-score/percentile), all context features
available at event time (Part 4)`.

## Triple-barrier labeling (LOCKED, per user specification — unchanged)

| Config | Target | Stop | Max hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CONFIG_A** | 5 pts | 5 pts | 10 min |
| **CONFIG_B** | 10 pts | 5 pts | 15 min |
| **CONFIG_C** | 5 pts | 10 pts | 15 min |

### Barrier-resolution algorithm (LOCKED — conservative, no invented order)
Walking forward snapshot-by-snapshot from the event timestamp: for each step
`[spot_i, spot_{i+1}]`, let `lo, hi = min/max` of the two endpoints and
`band = |target_level - stop_level|`. **AMBIGUOUS** fires on either of two
independent conditions:
1. Both target and stop levels fall within `[lo, hi]` (both crossed, by the
   intermediate value theorem — order still unknowable from two points).
2. `|spot_{i+1} - spot_i| ≥ band` **and** exactly one level falls within
   `[lo, hi]`. This is the condition that actually matters in practice: given
   target/stop are placed symmetrically around the entry, condition 1 is
   almost structurally unreachable on a live walk's first resolving step (the
   walk always starts strictly inside the band), so relying on it alone
   would silently under-flag the real risk — a single-endpoint "resolution"
   on an unusually large 2-minute jump cannot rule out the true path having
   touched the *other* barrier first and reversed, entirely invisible
   between two snapshots. **This was found and fixed during unit-testing of
   the algorithm, before it touched real data** (disclosed per the project's
   look-ahead-bug precedent, not discovered after peeking at outcomes).
- Otherwise (exactly one level in range, jump below band width) → that
  barrier resolves at `snapshot_{i+1}`'s timestamp.
- Neither level in range → continue; update running MFE/MAE from `lo/hi`.
- Max holding period elapsed unresolved → **TIMEOUT**.

**AMBIGUOUS is a terminal, unconverted outcome** — never counted as a win,
never dropped silently. Its rate is reported per config as a headline
statistic (per user confirmation: run CONFIG_A/B/C exactly as specified and
report the AMBIGUOUS rate prominently rather than adjusting thresholds to
avoid it).

### Non-overlapping event selection (LOCKED — added at Phase 5, before any
### barrier outcome was computed, on a data-quality basis only)
Phase 5's event detection produced 53,719 base and 6,592 combined (E6) event
firings over 58 days — far more than one independent observation per
occurrence, because a threshold crossing (e.g. a volume z-score staying
above 2.5) typically persists across several consecutive ~2-min snapshots.
Labeling every firing would pseudo-replicate the same underlying price path
dozens of times and silently inflate every downstream sample-size and
significance statistic. **Locked rule:** for a given (event_type,
relative_strike, option_type, barrier_config), once an instance fires and
enters its triple-barrier holding window, no new instance of that same
series may fire again until the current one resolves (TARGET_FIRST /
STOP_FIRST / TIMEOUT / AMBIGUOUS). This is the standard non-overlapping
triple-barrier convention (de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine
Learning*) and is applied **per config independently** (a 10-minute hold and
a 15-minute hold overlap differently). The full raw event log is retained
undiminished for descriptive/exploratory reporting; only the non-overlapping
subset is triple-barrier labeled and used for every candidate statistic
(win rate, mean return, HAC t-stat, FDR, SPA).

**Known consequence, stated before running:** on a sampled day, 45% of 2-min
intervals move ≥5 pts and 19% move ≥10 pts — CONFIG_A's AMBIGUOUS rate is
expected to be **large**. This is an honest resolution limit of the only
available data, not a defect to be engineered away.

Stored per event: `event_time, entry_spot, target_level, stop_level,
first_barrier, barrier_hit_time, maximum_favorable_excursion,
maximum_adverse_excursion`.

## Context features (Part 4, versioned `context_features_v1.0`)
Spot: 2/5/10-min return, same-day realized vol (causal), distance from
session VWAP, distance from intraday high/low so far, opening-range state.
Option: ATM CE/PE volume anomaly, CE/PE volume imbalance, ATM CE/PE OI
change, PCR(OI) and its change, OI concentration (top-3 strikes' share of
total OI), nearest OI wall above/below spot and distance to it, CE/PE premium
momentum, IV skew (CE IV − PE IV at ATM, when both solvable). VIX: level,
change, momentum, acceleration, same-day percentile-so-far. Time: minute of
day, time bucket (30-min), day of week, calendar days to expiry, expiry-day
flag. All are computed strictly from data at or before event time.

## Models (Part 7, LOCKED)
Environment note: neither LightGBM nor XGBoost was installed in this
environment; **`lightgbm` was added** (`requirements.txt`, `lightgbm>=4.7`) —
matches this repo's existing precedent of installing a new dependency per
experiment (`arch` for Exp013, `hmmlearn` for Exp017).

- **MODEL_A** — price + time features only.
- **MODEL_B** — MODEL_A + India VIX features.
- **MODEL_C** — MODEL_B + full option-event feature set (E1–E5 raw
  statistics for the 5×2 tracked series, PCR, OI concentration, OI-wall
  distances, premium momentum, IV skew).
- All three: `lightgbm.LGBMClassifier(objective="multiclass")` over 3 classes
  (`TARGET_FIRST`, `STOP_FIRST`, `TIMEOUT`). **AMBIGUOUS events are excluded
  from model training and scoring** (no resolved label exists) but their rate
  is reported per fold as a data-quality statistic, never imputed.
- Calibration: `sklearn.calibration.CalibratedClassifierCV` (isotonic) fit on
  each fold's validation split. Metrics: Brier score, log loss, reliability
  by decile — reported per model, per fold, per config.
- **Primary question:** does MODEL_C beat MODEL_B beats MODEL_A out-of-sample
  (Brier/log-loss), not "is MODEL_C accurate in isolation."

## Walk-forward (Part 8, LOCKED)
`nifty_quant.research.walkforward.generate_windows()` only supports a 2-way
(train/test) split over **calendar** days — it has no validation concept and
no trading-day awareness. Reused as-is for the outer cut, not
re-implemented: `generate_windows(start=2026-06-22, end=<latest>,
train_days=47, test_days=10, step_days=10)`. (Originally locked as
33/8/8 calendar days on the mistaken assumption of ~1 trading day per
calendar day; at this sample's actual ~0.72 trading-day/calendar-day density
that left only ~23 trading days for a 25-day fit split — zero left for
calibration. Corrected during the Phase 8 dry run, before any model touched
real data; an implementation bug fix, not a threshold change — the
trading-day targets below are unchanged.) The returned `train_start/train_end`
calendar range is then split, in EXP033's own fold-assembly code (a plain
list slice, not new fold-boundary logic), into the **first 25 trading days
present in the panel** within that range for model fitting and the
**remaining trading days** (≈8) for calibration (`CalibratedClassifierCV`)
— never touched by model fitting. `test_start/test_end` is the held-out
out-of-sample period, scored once, never re-visited. At 58 usable days this
yields 3 folds now; the same unchanged fold formula re-runs as more days
accumulate — exactly Exp032's "re-run unchanged, let the sample grow"
discipline. Per fold: train on strictly past data, freeze the model, predict
the test period, record every event, aggregate only after all folds
complete.

## Power analysis (read before any result, LOCKED)
At 58 usable days, an E6 event firing on, say, 15–25% of days yields on the
order of **10–20 tradeable observations per (event, config) cell** — smaller
than Exp032's weakest candidates (S5/S6 at n=11–12), which needed apparent
Sharpe ≥7.5 for significance. **Expect most of the 18 primary hypotheses
(6 E6 events × 3 configs) to land INCONCLUSIVE by construction.** A
significant result here is a trigger for adversarial code review before it
is a trigger for celebration, exactly per Exp032's rule.

## Signal generation rule (Part 9, LOCKED)
A candidate event fires a signal only if **all** hold:
1. Event detected (E6 trigger, or model-only path — see below).
2. Historical support in the training fold ≥ **MIN_SUPPORT = 30** occurrences.
3. Calibrated `P_TARGET_FIRST ≥ 0.55` **and** `P_TARGET_FIRST − P_STOP_FIRST ≥ 0.15`.
4. Expected value > 0 net of the futures-proxy cost schedule above.
5. Fold-level calibration acceptable: Brier(MODEL_C) < Brier(naive base-rate
   model) on that fold's test split.
6. Signal is generated **out-of-sample** (test fold only — never on the
   training/validation data that produced the model).

Any failure → **NO_TRADE**. Thresholds above are fixed now; if alternate
thresholds are ever explored, they are additional hypotheses subject to the
same FDR correction, never a post-hoc best-of-N pick (Part 9's explicit
prohibition).

## Multiple-testing control (Part 17, LOCKED)
**Primary hypothesis universe: 18** (6 E6 combined events × 3 barrier
configs). Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at **q=0.10**
(`statsmodels.stats.multitest.multipletests`, same as Exp032) applied across
all 18 primary p-values. Hansen SPA / White Reality Check
(`nifty_quant.analytics.data_snooping.reality_check_spa`, block=10, n_boot=5000)
applied to the aligned (day × 18) net-return matrix. **Every one of the 18 is
reported, including failures — none may be dropped after the fact.**

## Decision rules (LOCKED, applied mechanically)
- **REJECT** — mean net return < 0 and (HAC t ≤ −1.0 or win rate < 45%).
- **COST-FRAGILE** — modifier: positive at ×1.0 spread, negative at ×2.0
  (reusing Exp032's `SPREAD_MULTS` sensitivity sweep).
- **UNSTABLE** — modifier: sign of mean return differs between the first and
  second half of the walk-forward test folds.
- **CANDIDATE FOR FORWARD TESTING** (max attainable) — mean net > 0, survives
  BH at q=0.10, SPA p < 0.10, not COST-FRAGILE, calibration acceptable.
  Mandatory follow-up: adversarial look-ahead review before anything else.
- **INCONCLUSIVE — CONTINUE COLLECTING** — mean net > 0 but fails BH or SPA.
  Expected for most of the 18 given the power analysis above.
- **ARCHIVE** — mean net ≈ 0 (\|t\| < 0.5), no structural reason to persist.

No candidate may be promoted to live or paper trading by this phase.
